About the gym

Built for people who actually train.

Tohlda Fitness Gym Bagumbong is a wide, open-plan training floor above Bagumbong Road — right beside Young Achievers' School. No queues for the bench, no gimmicks, no lock-in contracts you have to argue your way out of.

The floor is laid out the way lifters want it: heavy free weights and racks on the rubber-matted side, plate-loaded and selectorized machines through the middle, cable and functional trainers by the windows, and cardio near the front desk. High ceilings, industrial fans and full-length windows keep it breathable even in Manila heat.

Whether you're on your first month or your tenth year under the bar, you get the same thing: complete equipment, room to breathe, and a crowd that puts the weights back.

What's inside

Everything you need under one roof.

Complete kit for strength, hypertrophy and conditioning — plus the boring essentials that make a gym usable every day.

Free Weights & Racks

Olympic barbells, full dumbbell rack, flat / incline / decline benches, squat and power racks over rubber matting.

Plate-Loaded Machines

Chest press, shoulder press, rows, pulldowns, leg press, hack squat, leg extension and curl — the full push/pull/legs spread.

Before you come

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book ahead?

No. Tohlda Bagumbong is walk-in — just show up during opening hours, pay at the front desk and train. Booking is only needed for personal training sessions.

Is there a joining or registration fee?

No joining fee and no lock-in contract. You pay for a walk-in session or a membership period, and that's it.

What should I bring on my first visit?

Training clothes, closed rubber shoes, a towel and a water bottle (there's a free purified water dispenser on the floor). A padlock if you want to use a locker.

I've never lifted before. Will I be out of place?

Not at all. Ask at the front desk for a free orientation on your first visit — someone will walk you through the layout and show you how the machines work. Personal training is available if you want a full program.

Do you have showers and lockers?

Yes — a changing area with steel lockers is on the same floor. Bring your own padlock and towel.

Can I pause or transfer my membership?

Transfers and pauses are handled case by case at the front desk. Message the Facebook page or ask in person and the staff will sort it out with you.

Do you accept GCash or card?

Ask the front desk for current payment options — cash is always accepted.

Is there another Tohlda branch?

Yes, there's also a Tohlda Fitness Gym in Novaliches, Quezon City. Membership at one branch doesn't automatically cover the other — check with staff.
