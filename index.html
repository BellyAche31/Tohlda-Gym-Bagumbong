About the gym

Built for people who actually train.

Tohlda Fitness Gym Bagumbong is a wide, open-plan training floor above Bagumbong Road — right beside Young Achievers' School. No queues for the bench, no gimmicks, no lock-in contracts you have to argue your way out of.

The floor is laid out the way lifters want it: heavy free weights and racks on the rubber-matted side, plate-loaded and selectorized machines through the middle, cable and functional trainers by the windows, and cardio near the front desk. High ceilings, industrial fans and full-length windows keep it breathable even in Manila heat.

Whether you're on your first month or your tenth year under the bar, you get the same thing: complete equipment, room to breathe, and a crowd that puts the weights back.

What's inside

Everything you need under one roof.

Complete kit for strength, hypertrophy and conditioning — plus the boring essentials that make a gym usable every day.

Free Weights & Racks

Olympic barbells, full dumbbell rack, flat / incline / decline benches, squat and power racks over rubber matting — plus a marked deadlift area in the open-air annex.

Plate-Loaded Machines

Chest press, shoulder press, rows, pulldowns, leg press, hack squat, leg extension and curl — the full push/pull/legs spread.

Dual cable crossover, lat pulldown, seated row and a functional trainer with a full attachment rack.

SciFit upright bikes, treadmills and steppers near the front — good for warm-ups, conditioning finishers and steady-state work.

Secure steel lockers, changing area and a seating bench so you're not living out of your gym bag on the floor.

Refuel Station

Chilled drinks, protein shakes and supplements at the front desk. Unlimited purified water from the floor dispenser for ₱10 — members and non-members alike.

Clean & Monitored

CCTV coverage, posted gym rules, fire extinguishers and a wipe-down-after-use policy everyone actually follows.

Personal Training

Coaches for beginners, strength blocks, fat-loss programs and competition prep. Bundles from 1 to 15 sessions.

Free WiFi

Free WiFi throughout the floor — stream your playlist, log your sets or take a call between rounds. Ask the desk for the password.

Before you come

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book ahead?

No. Tohlda Bagumbong is walk-in — just show up during opening hours, pay at the front desk and train. Booking is only needed for personal training sessions.

Is there a joining or registration fee?

Only if you want the member rates. A one-time ₱300 annual membership unlocks them, and it's waived entirely if you take the 3-month or 6-month rate. You can also skip it completely and train at the non-member rate — ₱100 per session or ₱950 a month. Either way there's no lock-in contract.

What should I bring on my first visit?

Training clothes, closed rubber shoes, a towel and a water bottle — unlimited purified water from the dispenser is ₱10 at the front desk. Bring a padlock if you want to use a locker.

I've never lifted before. Will I be out of place?

Not at all — the crowd here ranges from first-timers to people who've been lifting for years. Ask the front desk if you're unsure how a machine works, and if you'd like a full program, personal training starts at ₱300 for a single session.

Do you have lockers?

Yes — 24 numbered steel lockers with a changing area and seating bench, on the same floor. Bring your own padlock, and clear your locker before you leave.

Is there WiFi?

Yes, free WiFi for everyone training. Ask the front desk for the password.

Can I pause or transfer my membership?

Transfers and pauses are handled case by case at the front desk. Message the Facebook page or ask in person and the staff will sort it out with you.

Do you accept GCash or card?

Ask the front desk for current payment options — cash is always accepted.

Is there another Tohlda branch?

Yes, there's also a Tohlda Fitness Gym in Novaliches, Quezon City. Membership at one branch doesn't automatically cover the other — check with staff.

House rules

  1. Don't drop the weights.
  2. Return weights after use.
  3. Wipe equipment after use.
  4. Ask for a spotter when lifting heavy.
  5. Share equipment — allow others to work in between sets.
  6. Wear appropriate gym attire and shoes.
  7. Report any broken or damaged equipment.
  8. Clear your locker when you leave.
